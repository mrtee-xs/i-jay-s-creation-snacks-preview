Culinary Integrity

Hygiene & Hot-Transit Standards

We treat every celebration with uncompromising commercial hygiene standards to ensure your guests enjoy piping-hot, crispy finger foods.

Standard 01

65°C Thermo-Insulated Transit

All party platters and individual snack packs are transferred immediately from draining racks into pre-warmed commercial insulated carriers, keeping internal food temperatures above 65°C during transit.

  • Commercial thermal insulated courier carriers
  • Steam-vented packaging prevents pastry condensation
  • Guaranteed hot arrival at your venue
Standard 02

Single-Cycle Oil Purity

We exclusively use fresh, single-cycle refined sunflower and vegetable oils. Burnt or degraded oils are discarded daily to ensure clean, crisp pastries without greasy aftertastes.

  • 100% Zero-trans-fat culinary frying oils
  • Digital thermometer controlled frying temperatures (180°C)
  • Zero stale oil recycling or re-use
Standard 03

Morning-Fresh Preparation

Every spring roll wrapper is rolled thin and stuffed on the morning of your event. Strictly zero pre-fried or frozen leftover inventory is ever served.

  • Fresh local poultry and prime lean beef fillings
  • Crispy golden crusts that retain texture for hours
  • Handmade sweet yeast puff-puff fried fresh to order
Standard 04

Tamper-Evident Food Packaging

Whether you choose individual guest packs or grand display trays, every order is secured with food-grade security seals that verify pristine chain of custody.

  • Food-contact certified kraft boxes with clear PET windows
  • Tamper-evident serialized security tape
  • Includes compostable cocktail napkins and toothpicks

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about order lead times, minimum quantities, packaging, and custom menus.

How far in advance should I book my event?

For small party orders (25 to 50 packs), 24 hours advance notice is recommended. For grand banquets and weddings (100 to 500+ packs), we advise booking 3 to 7 days ahead to secure your morning frying slot.

Can we customize the items inside each pack?

Yes. You can swap or add items such as spicy asun, barbecued chicken, jumbo prawns in batter, or mini quiches to suit your guests' specific dietary preferences.

How do you ensure the pastries arrive crispy and hot?

We fry in timed batches directly before scheduled dispatch and transport in specialized, double-layer thermo-insulated thermal bags that vent excess moisture while preserving core heat above 65°C.

Do you provide on-site catering staff for live frying?

Yes. For luxury weddings and executive galas requiring live on-site frying stations with uniformed service chefs, we provide mobile frying setups upon advance reservation.
